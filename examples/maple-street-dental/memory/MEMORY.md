# Memory — Maple Street Dental (SAMPLE, fictional)

## Facts that stay true

- Hours: Mon–Thu 8:00–17:00, Fri 8:00–14:00. Closed weekends.
- Address: 12 Maple Street. Free parking behind the building.
- New patients: first visit is a cleaning plus exam; arrive 10 minutes
  early for forms.
- Insurance: we bill most major insurers directly; patients confirm
  coverage with their insurer before treatment.
- Reminders go out 48 hours before an appointment, once.

## Decisions

- Day 1 — Reminder messages are drafted by front-desk and approved by the
  Office Manager every morning. No automatic sending.
- Day 1 — Billing questions are never answered by an AI lane. They are
  flagged for a human the same day.

## Lane notes

- front-desk: friendly, short messages. Never discuss treatment or
  diagnoses — that is clinical, hand it to a human.
- faq: answers come only from the approved patient information sheets.
  If it is not in a sheet, the answer is "let me check with the team".
- billing-watch: flags only. It never writes to a patient, ever.
