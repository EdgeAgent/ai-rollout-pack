# The Memory Folder

An AI employee has no memory of its own. What it "knows" about your
business is whatever is written in a folder of plain files — files you
own, can read, can correct, and can take with you if you change tools.

**The folder is the asset.** Models change, vendors change, subscriptions
lapse. The folder is the business's knowledge, written down once and
reused every day. Building it feels like paperwork; it is actually the
handover that never has to happen, because the documenting was a byproduct
of the doing.

## Structure

```
memory/
  MEMORY.md          the facts: hours, prices, policies, decisions
  lanes/
    front-desk.md    how the front-desk lane works, its playbook
    <lane>.md        one playbook per lane
  corrections.md     every human correction, dated (or a section in MEMORY.md)
```

## The three rules of the folder

### 1. If it is not written, it is not known

A price mentioned in a chat, a policy decided in a hallway — the AI
employee will not know them tomorrow. Facts and decisions go into the
folder the day they happen. This is the single habit that separates an AI
employee that compounds in value from one that re-learns your business
every morning.

### 2. Corrections live in the folder, not in chat

When the AI employee gets something wrong, fix the output — then fix the
folder. "We never offer discounts on the phone", written once in
`MEMORY.md`, prevents a hundred wrong drafts. A correction left in a chat
window prevents nothing.

### 3. Decisions are dated and outrank chatter

Record decisions with a date: who decided, what, and why in one line.
When an old instruction and a dated decision disagree, the dated decision
wins — and the folder is where anyone can check which is which.

## What a good MEMORY.md looks like

- Short. Facts, not essays. If a fact takes a paragraph, it is probably a
  policy — give it its own line under Policies.
- Current. A wrong fact in memory is worse than a missing one: the AI
  employee will use it confidently. Review the folder at the month
  review, the way you would review any business record.
- Boring. The best memory folders read like a good operations manual,
  because that is exactly what they are.

## Ownership

Back the folder up with the rest of the business's records. If the AI
tool is cancelled, replaced, or outgrown, the folder stays — and the next
tool, or the next hire, starts from everything the business already
learned.
