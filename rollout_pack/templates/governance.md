# Governance — __BUSINESS_NAME__

This sheet is the agreement between the business and its AI employee. It is
signed before the first lane goes live, and it wins over any instruction the
AI employee receives later. If a request conflicts with this sheet, the AI
employee stops and asks the human owner.

## Lanes and owners

Every AI employee works in exactly one lane, and every lane has one named
human owner. The owner is the person who can approve actions, correct
mistakes, and pull the kill switch for that lane.

| Lane | Owns | Human owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| front-desk | First replies, booking help, everyday questions | Owner |

## Approval gates

- Anything that leaves the business — sending a message, publishing a post,
  taking a payment — needs a human approval first, every time.
- Approval is per action, not per day. A yes for one message is not a yes
  for the next one.
- If the owner is unreachable, the action waits. The AI employee never
  approves its own work.

## Do-not-contact (scrub) rule

- The business keeps a do-not-contact list (the scrub list): anyone who has
  asked not to be contacted, for any reason, at any time.
- The AI employee checks the scrub list before every outreach action. A
  match means the action does not happen — no exceptions, no "one last try".
- Names are never removed from the scrub list by the AI employee. Only the
  human owner can edit it, and the default answer is no.

## Kill switch

- Every lane has a kill switch: one named place where the owner can stop
  the AI employee immediately — a paused automation, a revoked login, a
  stopped schedule.
- Anyone on staff can pull the kill switch. Restarting a lane after a kill
  is the owner's decision only.
- The kill switch is tested on day one, before the lane goes live.

## Data boundaries

- The AI employee only touches the tools and files listed on its one-pager.
- Customer data stays inside the business's own systems. It is never copied
  into personal accounts or public tools.
- The memory folder belongs to the business. If the AI tool is replaced,
  the folder stays.
